# ATLAW — Personal Injury Website

## Overview

ATLAW (AT Law Group, PLLC) is a Dearborn, Michigan law firm founded in 2013 by Dewnya Bazzi. The firm handles multiple practice areas but has made a strategic decision to market exclusively around **personal injury**. This website is the PI-focused public face of the firm — authority-building for Dewnya, lead generation for injury cases, and SEO-driven city/keyword landing pages across Southeast Michigan.

The site is modeled after mikemorris.com: a single authority landing page for the founder, funneling into the PI firm's services and intake flow.

## Goals

1. Position Dewnya Bazzi as the authority in Michigan personal injury law.
2. Generate qualified PI leads through SEO-optimized landing pages.
3. Convert visitors into consultations via clear CTAs and intake forms.
4. Communicate "unreasonable hospitality" — warm, strategic, client-centered tone.
5. Build city-specific and keyword-specific landing pages for organic search.
6. Deliver a fast, modern, mobile-first experience.

## Core User Flow

1. Visitor lands on homepage (organic search, referral, or direct).
2. Homepage builds trust: Dewnya's story, firm credibility, social proof.
3. Visitor navigates to their injury type or city-specific page.
4. Visitor reads relevant PI content, sees case results and testimonials.
5. Visitor clicks CTA → intake form (Typeform) or calls the firm.
6. Secondary: visitor explores team, about, or contact pages.

## Site Structure (PI-Only)

### Primary Pages
- **Homepage** — Authority landing page. Dewnya as the face. Hero, story, social proof, PI focus, CTA.
- **Practice Areas Landing** — Personal injury sub-areas overview.
- **Personal Injury** — Core PI page with sub-practice links.
- **Auto Accidents** — Michigan No-Fault / PIP benefits focus.
- **Medical Malpractice** — Serious negligence cases.
- **Wrongful Death** — Loss and accountability.
- **Premises Liability / Slip and Fall** — Unsafe property conditions.
- **Dog Bite Injuries** — Animal attack cases.
- **Workers' Compensation** — On-the-job injuries.

### SEO Landing Pages (per practice area)
- City-specific pages: Ann Arbor, Dearborn, Detroit, Dearborn Heights, Wayne County, Oakland County, Macomb County.
- Keyword-specific pages under each city (e.g., "bike accident lawyer Ann Arbor").

### Supporting Pages
- **About / Dewnya's Story** — Founder narrative, mission, values.
- **Our Team** — Attorney and key staff profiles.
- **Contact** — Office info, intake form embed, phone, map.
- **Privacy Policy** — Michigan-compliant.
- **Terms of Use** — Attorney advertising disclaimers.

### Not on This Site
- Other practice areas (business law, criminal defense, estate planning, immigration, tax, etc.) — these are backend/internal only.
- Visionary Builders / coaching — separate project, not part of this build.
- Global Reach / Dubai / Manila — not relevant to PI marketing.
- News & Insights — defer unless client requests.
