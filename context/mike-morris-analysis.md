## 04 — FAQ / Subtopic Page (e.g. Rear-End Accident Lawyers)

### Structure (top → bottom)
1. **Hero banner** — Practice-specific H1 ("Rear-End Accident Lawyers"), breadcrumb, red CTA "START MY FREE CASE REVIEW"
2. **Sub-nav tabs** — OVERVIEW | STEPS TO TAKE | CLIENT TESTIMONIALS | FAQ | RELATED CASES. Active tab red-underlined. Keeps user on page.
3. **Content + sticky sidebar** — Left: long-form article with H2s, attorney photo cutout. Right: sticky "3 Ways to Start Your Case" card (Call / Email / Calculate Settlement).
4. **Results carousel** — Dark bg. "They Offered Less. We Fought for More." Client photos + insurance offer vs actual recovery ($1.5M → $9.6M, $250K → $2.84M, etc.). Left/right arrows.
5. **Steps section** — "What to Do After a Rear-End Accident" — 4 cards with lifestyle photos, numbered steps.
6. **No-fees banner** — Red gradient strip: "NO FEES. NO RISK. YOU ONLY PAY WHEN WE WIN"
7. **Reviews section** — "Backed by 5,000+ Five-Star Reviews" — 3 review cards, star ratings, real names + dates, "Show More" links.
8. **FAQ accordion** — Clean rows, one question each, chevron toggle. ~9 questions.
9. **Case results ticker** — Horizontal scrolling red banner with dollar amounts + case type + county.
10. **Similar Cases carousel** — Card carousel linking related practice pages (Car Accidents, Truck, etc.)
11. **Long-form content** — "How to Protect Yourself From Lowballing Insurance Companies" with inline intake form sidebar.
12. **Full intake form** — Dark photo overlay (attorney bg), "See How Much We Can Win for You", First/Last/Phone/Email/What Happened + red CTA.
13. **Additional Resources** — 2-column link grid.
14. **Footer** — Dark navy. 11 office locations grid. Newsletter signup. Practice Areas list. Contact info.

## Cross-Page Design Patterns

### Conversion Architecture
- **Sticky sidebar intake form** on every content page — always visible during scroll
- **Multiple CTA touchpoints** — hero, mid-content, bottom. Never >2 scrolls from a conversion point
- **"3 Ways to Start"** card — Call / Email / Calculate Settlement — gives user choice of channel
- **Dark overlay intake form** — full-width section, attorney photo bg, premium feel
- **Phone number prominence** — vanity number (855-MIKE-WINS), displayed 3-5× per page
- **Form fields** — First, Last, Phone, Email, "What happened? (optional)". Minimal friction.

### Social Proof System
- **Results carousel** — "They Offered Less. We Fought for More." Insurance offer vs recovery. Client photos + first name/last initial. Dark bg.
- **Reviews carousel** — "Backed by 5,000+ Five-Star Reviews" with star ratings, real names, dates
- **Stats bar** — 4 big numbers ($2B+, 250 experts, 100K+ clients, 35 years)
- **Case results ticker** — Horizontal red banner scrolling amounts + case types + counties
- **Award badges** — Expertise.com, scattered through content

### Content Strategy
- **Tab navigation** (FAQ/topic pages only) — OVERVIEW | STEPS | TESTIMONIALS | FAQ | RELATED — keeps user on-page
- **FAQ accordion** — Clean, one question per row, chevron icon. Appears on location + topic pages.
- **Long-form SEO content** — 1500+ words, localized per city/neighborhood
- **Internal linking everywhere** — "Similar Cases" carousel, "Other Practice Areas" carousel, "Additional Resources" grid, breadcrumbs
- **Educational framing** — "What is a Personal Injury Claim?" — positions firm as authority
- **Steps/process** — Numbered step cards showing what to do after accident. Builds trust through transparency.

### Visual Design System
- **Red (#CC0000)** — primary action color. All CTAs, buttons, banners, active states.
- **Dark charcoal/near-black** — authority sections (results carousel, intake overlay, footer). Contrast with white content.
- **White** — main content areas. Clean, readable.
- **Red gradient banners** — "NO FEES. NO RISK." and "Get a FREE Case Evaluation" use diagonal red gradient strips with dot texture.
- **Typography** — Bold serif for H1/H2 headlines (red), sans-serif for body. All caps for stats/labels.
- **Attorney photos** — Mike Morse cutout appears 2-3× per page (hero, content, intake bg). Builds personal brand.
- **Client photos** — in results carousel. Real faces = real trust.
- **Card patterns** — Rounded corners, subtle shadow. Used for practice types, reviews, results, steps.

### Footer Pattern
- **Multi-office grid** — Dark bg, city photo behind each card. Office name, address, phone, directions.
- **Newsletter signup** — Email + red "SUBSCRIBE" button.
- **3-column info** — Practice Areas list, Contact details, quick links.
- **Legal** — Copyright, Privacy Policy, Terms, Sitemap, "Site by Ciipey".
